Under the Hood

Five-stage psychoacoustic & entropy pipeline

Compiled to a single zero-dependency Rust binary (calf-codec).

01 · TRANSFORM

Vorbis-Windowed Fast MDCT

O(N log N) 2048/512-point MDCT with analytically exact Princen-Bradley TDAC (<1e-5 error), -18 dB/oct sidelobes, and variable long/short block switching on transients.

02 · PERCEPTION

24-Band Bark Masking

Models human cochlear critical bands, Terhardt hearing thresholds, asymmetric spreading, and tonal vs. noise flatness.

03 · STEREO

Joint M/S + Intensity Stereo

Full Mid/Side stereo in lows/mids; switches to Mid + 5-bit pan angles above 2.8–6.4 kHz to halve stereo bit cost.

04 · HIGHS & TEXTURE

HBE + Perceptual Noise Fill

Folds lower-octave harmonics into crisp 10.5–22 kHz highs and injects energy-matched noise to kill metallic artifacts.

05 · ENTROPY

0.75 Companding + 32-bit rANS

Dead-zone non-uniform quantization packed into 64-frame rANS superblocks with temporal delta-coded envelopes.
